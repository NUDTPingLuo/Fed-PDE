# Data setup

Antiderivative, Burgers and diffusion–reaction samples are generated by
`operator_tasks/tasks.py`; no download is needed. Experiment seeds and solver
settings are in the corresponding YAML files and archived result metadata.

Public data are obtained from the original providers:

- PDEBench: https://doi.org/10.18419/darus-2986
- CFDBench: https://github.com/luo-yining/CFDBench
- OpenFWI: https://openfwi-lanl.github.io/

Place the files in this layout, or edit the paths in `config/real_*.yaml`:

```text
data/public/
├── PDEBench/
│   ├── 2D_DarcyFlow_beta0.01_Train.hdf5
│   └── 2D_rdb_NA_NA.h5
├── CFDBench/cylinder/
│   ├── bc/case*/
│   ├── geo/case*/
│   └── prop/case*/
└── OpenFWI/
    ├── curvefault_b_train.txt
    ├── curvefault_b_val.txt
    └── CurveFault_B/
        ├── seis*.npy
        └── vel*.npy
```

Keep the original CFDBench case directories and filenames. Use the official
CurveFault-B training and validation annotation files in their original order;
the loader resolves their data/model pairs relative to `data_root`, including
the basenames of original absolute paths. This study uses the first 2,000
training and first 500 validation examples; the latter are reported as test
examples. Preserve the annotated filenames when downloading OpenFWI arrays.

The public split, preprocessing, temporal horizons and response-codebook seed
are fixed by the YAML configurations. Large raw data are not included here.
